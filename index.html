How it works
  1. Import the card statement CSV, or add cash and personal-card expenses by hand.
  2. Mark each charge Business or Personal.
  3. Describe each business charge and who it was with.
  4. Attach a receipt: drop it on the charge, or in the tray to have it matched.
  5. Submit. Danna gets an email from you with the report and receipts attached. A copy stays in your Sent Items.

Send to Danna
